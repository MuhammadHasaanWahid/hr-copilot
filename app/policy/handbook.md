# Global Leave, Attendance & Data Policy (fictional, v4.2)

[LV-1.1] Annual leave entitlement. Employees receive 22 working days of annual leave per calendar year, rising to 25 days after five years of continuous service. Entitlement is recorded in the leave management system and is the authoritative balance.

[LV-1.2] Leave balance and pending requests. Available balance equals entitlement plus carried-over days minus days already taken minus days in pending requests. A request may not exceed the available balance; unpaid leave must be requested separately through HR.

[LV-2.1] Notice period. Requests of more than five working days must be submitted at least 14 calendar days before the first day of leave. Requests of five working days or fewer require at least 3 calendar days notice. Sick leave is exempt.

[LV-2.2] Approval levels. All leave requires approval by the employee's current reporting manager. Requests longer than ten working days additionally require approval from the department head.

[LV-3.1] Team coverage. Managers must keep at least 60 percent of their direct team available on every working day. Requests that would push coverage below this threshold may only be approved after the manager documents a coverage plan.

[LV-3.2] Resolving competing requests. When requests cannot all be approved, managers prioritise in this order: (a) business-critical role coverage, (b) employees whose untaken leave would otherwise be forfeited at year end, (c) the order in which requests were submitted. Managers should offer alternative dates rather than reject outright.

[LV-3.3] Project handover. An employee assigned to a project with a deadline inside the requested leave period must attach a handover note naming who covers their responsibilities.

[LV-3.4] Critical roles near critical deadlines. Holders of a critical role on a business-critical project may not be absent during the five working days up to and including the project deadline unless the department head grants an exception.

[LV-4.1] Carry-over and forfeiture. A maximum of five unused annual leave days carry over into the next calendar year. Days above this cap are forfeited on 31 December.

[LV-5.1] Approval routing. Leave approvals are routed to the reporting manager recorded in the HRIS. When an employee's reporting line changes, the workflow route and any pending approvals must be reassigned to the new manager within two business days. Changes to routing are performed by HR systems administration.

[AT-1.1] Absence monitoring. Absence analytics are performed on aggregated data only. No group smaller than five employees may be reported, and individual-level absence review requires a documented reason and is restricted to HR.

[AT-1.2] Absence follow-up. Where absence patterns indicate possible workload, wellbeing or engagement issues, HR should first engage managers and offer support measures (workload review, wellbeing check-ins, flexible working) before any formal process.

[DP-1.1] Data access. Employees may view their own records. Managers may view the non-compensation records of their direct reports. HR business partners may view all non-compensation records. Compensation data is restricted to HR administrators.

[DP-1.2] Automated decisions. No decision with legal or significant effect on an employee may be taken solely by an automated system. Automated tools may recommend; an accountable human decides.
